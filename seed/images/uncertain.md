# Image picks flagged as uncertain by the selection agents

Check these first when reviewing images (`./obbd media-sheet`, then open dist/media-review.html).

## Batch 1
- **ben-sira**: only the Masada casemate room where the scroll was found. No free image of the manuscript exists; consider leaving it empty.
- **behemoth**: Edfu relief of Horus spearing Seth as a hippopotamus. The link to Job is indirect.
- **ahaz**: Hezekiah's bulla ("son of Ahaz"). No free image of Ahaz's own seal impression was found.
- **asherah**: Judahite pillar figurine. Its link to Asherah is debated, and the caption says so.
- **avaris**: site photo shows little. The gallery image is a modern fresco reconstruction, and its caption says so.
- **akitu-festival**: shows Babylon's Processional Way, not the festival.
- **assyrian-siege-warfare**: the primary file has no stated source or museum, so the caption is generic.
- **ashdod**: uses the Ashdoda figurine, because the only site photo is just grass.
- **battle-of-carchemish**: the caption's British Museum location for ABC 5 comes from the agent's own knowledge, not the file description.
- **anat**: the caption names no museum.

## Batch 3
- **eloquent-peasant**: Papyrus Berlin 10499 (P. Ramesseum A). It's unclear which side carries the Peasant text, so the caption describes the papyrus as a whole.
- **erra-and-ishum**: plague amulet from Ashur. The caption's claim that it carries an Erra excerpt comes from the agent's knowledge of the genre, not the file description.
- **esarhaddon-prisms**: Nimrud prism (Iraq Museum), not a confirmed Nineveh A/B exemplar.
- **ezekiel-throne-chariot**: an interpretive pick, Ashur in the winged disc (Nimrud relief).
- **first-enoch**: Geʿez manuscript BL Or 485, identified only through Wikipedia's lead image. A Greek leaf is in the gallery.
- **etemenanki**: the primary photo is low resolution (600×400).
- **egyptian-rule-in-canaan**: the Seti I Beth-Shean stele caption gives no museum.
- **divine-warrior / divine-abandonment**: images reused from other entries (Baal stele; Esarhaddon's Black Stone).
- **deir-alla-inscription, diplomatic-marriage, esarhaddon-treaty-with-baal-of-tyre**: drawings or hand copies used as the primary image because no photograph was found.

## Batch 4

- **gudea**: the caption names the Louvre Ningishzida statue, taken from the file description only.
- **garden-of-god**: Mari Investiture painting, faded and photographed behind glass. Consider promoting the Ashurbanipal garden relief from the gallery.
- **great-hymn-to-shamash**: detail of the Sun God Tablet, used as an illustration.
- **goring-ox**: Hammurabi stele, because no Eshnunna tablet photo could be confirmed.
- **four-room-house**: modern model (Harvard Semitic Museum).
- **gaza**: 19th-century Frith photograph of the town.
- **herods-temple**: Robinson's Arch. The Holyland model is in the gallery and captioned as a model.
- **geshur**: et-Tell gate. Its Geshurite identification is hedged in the caption.
- **Empty**: hezekiah-bulla (no free photo of the Ophel bulla), jezebel (no free photo of the seal), the Hittite texts, and izbet-sartah-abecedary.

- **chaoskampf**: Layard's 1853 engraving of the Nimrud relief, not a photograph.

- **calendars-and-month-names**: Gezer Calendar, small in the frame.

- **canaanites**: gold-foiled bronze deity (MET), with no date or findspot in the description.
- **carchemish**: Yariri and Kamani relief (Ankara) rather than the site. The 1913 dig photo is in the gallery.

- **Empty**: community-rule.
