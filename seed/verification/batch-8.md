# Passage-tag verification: batch 8

Checked 78 entries (tabernacle through zoroastrianism; list in `.cache/digests/verify-8.txt`) against the WEB text with `./obbd check-passages --per 2`. All MT/English versification notes in the batch were checked and are correct. Six entries were changed: 7 tags changed, none removed, none added.

## Changed entries

- **table-of-nations**: `Isa 66:19` note "Tarshish, Put, Lud, Tubal and Javan as distant nations" → "Tarshish, Pul (read Put with LXX), Lud, Tubal and Javan as distant nations". MT reads *pûl*; Put is the LXX-based emendation, and the old note presented it as the text.
- **uzziah-epitaph**: `2Chr 26:23` rel `event` → `attests`; `2Kgs 15:7` rel `event` → `attests`. The plaque records a late Second Temple reburial of Uzziah's bones, not the original burial these verses narrate. It attests the person, not the same event.
- **winepresses**: `Isa 5:1-7` note "terracing, watchtower, hewn wine vat" → "clearing stones, watchtower, hewn wine vat". Isa 5:2 describes digging and removing stones, not terracing.
- **yehud-province**: `Neh 3:1-32` → `Neh 3:9-18`. The note is about the *pelek* districts, and that term occurs only in vv. 9, 12, 14–18.
- **yhwh-origins**: `Exod 3:13-15` note "Revelation of the divine name at Horeb in Midian" → "Divine name revealed at Horeb while Moses tends the flock of Jethro, priest of Midian". Exod 3:1 does not place Horeb in Midian; Moses leads the flock "to the back of the wilderness". The old note overclaimed for the Midianite hypothesis.
- **ziggurat**: `Gen 11:1-9` rel `setting` → `realia`. The entry is about a structure type (the ziggurat), not the place where the passage is set. The tower is a structure the passage presupposes.

## Flag for expert review

- **taharqa** `2Kgs 19:9`: the note calls the title "king" anachronistic for 701. That fits the standard chronology (Taharqa acceded in 690), but Kitchen and others argue that he could have led the army as a prince. Kept.
- **timna** `1Kgs 7:45-47` (`realia?`): the link from Solomon's bronze casting in the Jordan valley to Timna copper is weak. It now rests mainly on Ben-Yosef's 10th-century dating of the Arabah smelting. Kept because it is marked disputed and the note warns against "King Solomon's Mines".
- **tel-dan-stele** `2Sam 7:11-16` (`attests`): 2 Sam 7 speaks of David's "house" but has no exact *bêt dāwīd* formula. `language` or a looser note may fit better. `2Kgs 10:32-33` is tagged `event`, but the stele's campaign (c. 841) may come before the conquests summarised there.
- **tomb-of-rekhmire** `Ps 72:10-11` (`imagery`): this is a generic tribute-bearer comparison, the weakest tag in the entry.
- **theodotos-inscription** `Acts 2:5-11` (`practice`): linking the inscription's hostel for visitors from abroad to this passage is suggestive only.
- **temple-warning-inscription** `Mark 11:17`: "spoken in the court open to Gentiles" infers the location from the setting among the money-changers. The text says only "in the temple".
- **tell-tayinat** `1Kgs 7:1-12`: this passage is the palace description. It supports "temple within the palace complex" only through 7:12 and the wider layout.
- **ziggurat** `Ezek 43:13-17` and **ziggurat-and-babel** / **ziggurat** `Gen 28:12`: Albright's ziggurat-term comparisons (*ḥêq hāʾāreṣ*, *harʾēl*; *sullām*/*simmiltu*) are kept as disputed. Some specialists would drop them.
- **tell-al-rimah-stele**: the scope says this is "the first mention of Samaria by name in Assyrian royal inscriptions". This is often stated, but should be checked against earlier "Bit-Humri" references and the exact form *Samerināya*.
