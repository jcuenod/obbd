# Verification batch 2

Passage tags for 83 entries (bes to deir-alla-inscription) were checked against the WEB text. 13 entries were changed: 2 tags removed, 1 range extended, 1 range narrowed, 6 relations changed, 4 tags marked `disputed`, and 3 notes corrected.

## Changed entries

- **beth-shean**: `1Chr 10:10` was `setting`, note "Saul's head fastened in the temple of Dagon". Now `setting` with `disputed: true`, note "Saul's head in Dagon's temple; site unnamed, but Rowe assigned two Beth-shean temples to Dagon and Ashtaroth". Reason: Chronicles does not name Beth-shean, and the link depends on Rowe's identification of the Level V temples, which is now abandoned.
- **blessings-and-curses**: `Deut 27:11-26` note changed from "...'Amen' at Shechem" to "...'Amen', pronounced at Gerizim and Ebal". Reason: the passage names the two mountains, not Shechem.
- **byblos**: removed `Matt 1:1 | language` (Greek *biblos* 'book' from Byblos). Reason: an etymology that applies to any use of *biblos*; it says nothing about this passage.
- **care-for-the-dead**: `Sir 30:18` changed to `Sir 30:18-19`. The note changed from "Food set on a grave compared to offerings before an idol" to "Food laid on a grave likened to offerings to an idol that cannot eat". Reason: the comparison with idols is in v. 19, so the old range cut off the verse the note describes.
- **champion-combat**: `1Chr 20:5` rel changed from `attests` to `practice`. Reason: this is the Chronicler's version of a duel, not an attestation of a person or place.
- **chronology-of-kings**: `1Kgs 14:25-26` and `2Kgs 25:8` rel changed from `event` to `concept`. Reason: the entry is about the method of dating. It does not narrate these events; the passages serve as chronological anchors.
- **city-gates**: `1Kgs 22:10` rel changed from `setting` to `realia`. Reason: the open area at the gate is the physical feature in view. The entry is not the place where the passage is set.
- **constellations**: removed `Gen 15:5 | imagery` ("Abraham told to count the stars"). Reason: no named star or constellation appears, so the link is trivially weak.
- **corvee-labor**: `1Kgs 12:4, 18` rel changed from `event` to `practice`. Reason: the entry describes the institution, not a source for the Shechem assembly.
- **covenant-cutting-ceremony**: `Gen 15:9-21` narrowed to `Gen 15:9-18`. Reason: the rite and the covenant formula end at v. 18; vv. 19-21 only list the peoples of the land.
- **cult-reforms-archaeology**: added `disputed: true` to `2Kgs 18:3-6` (event), `2Kgs 23:4-20` (event) and `2Kgs 10:27` (practice, Lachish gate-shrine toilet). Reason: the entry's own author notes say the archaeological correlations (Arad, Beersheba, Lachish) are contested (Herzog vs Aharoni; Na'aman; Fried).
- **darius-the-mede**: `Dan 2:39; 7:5; 8:20` note changed from "A Median kingdom as second in Daniel's sequence of empires" to "Second kingdom usually read as Media (2:39; 7:5); 8:20 joins Media and Persia in one ram". Reason: Dan 8:20 does not present a separate Median kingdom.
- **debt-release**: `Jer 34:8-22` rel changed from `event` to `practice`. Reason: the comparison is with the institution of release edicts, not the same event.

## Flag for expert review

- **beth-shean** `1Kgs 14:25-26` (`attests`): Beth-shean does appear in Shoshenq's Karnak list, but the passage does not mention Beth-shean, and no relation fits well. Consider removing it or moving it to the shoshenq-i / bubastite-portal entries.
- **beth-shean** `1Chr 10:10`: kept as a pointer to the history of the Rowe identification. Remove it if this is judged too indirect.
- **book-of-the-dead** `Ps 15:1-5`, `Ps 24:3-6` (`parallel`): entrance liturgies compared with Spell 125. Egyptian temple-entrance purity inscriptions may be the closer analogue. Consider `disputed`.
- **black-obelisk** `2Kgs 9:1-10:36` (`attests`): the range covers the whole Jehu narrative. It is kept because Jehu as king is what is attested, but it could be narrowed (e.g. to 2Kgs 9:1-13; 10:28-36).
- **cult-reforms-archaeology**: the other event tags (`2Kgs 18:22`, `2Chr 29-31`, `2Chr 34:3-7`) were left unmarked. Decide whether the whole entry should treat the reform-archaeology link as disputed.
- **corvee-labor** `1Kgs 4:6`, `2Sam 20:24` (`attests`): kept, on the assumption that the entry covers epigraphic attestation of the office "over the forced labour" (e.g. the unprovenanced seal of Pelayahu). If it does not, change to `practice`.
- **david** `1Kgs 2:10-11` (`attests`) and **darius-the-mede** Daniel tags (`setting`): the relations are imperfect but defensible, so they were left as they are.
- **carchemish** `2Kgs 23:29`: the 609 campaign ended at Harran rather than Carchemish. The passage does not name Carchemish; it is kept as background via `2Chr 35:20`.
