# Passage-tag verification: batch 3

83 entries checked (demons … four-room-house) against the WEB text via `./obbd check-passages`.
10 entries changed: 9 tags edited, 1 tag removed, 0 added. The other 73 entries were left unchanged.

## Changes

- **diplomatic-gift-exchange**: `1Kgs 10:24-25` note "Kings bring yearly gifts to Solomon" → "Visitors from 'all the earth' bring yearly gifts (minḥâ) to Solomon". 1 Kgs 10:24 has "all the earth"; "kings of the earth" is the Chronicler's wording (2 Chr 9:23).
- **dreams-and-dream-interpretation**: `Num 12:6` → `Num 12:6-8`. The note's "Moses face to face" contrast is in vv. 7-8, so the old range cut it off.
- **east-india-house-inscription**: `Jer 51:58` rel `setting` → `realia`. The tag is about Babylon's broad walls as a structure the inscription describes building.
- **elyon**: `Ps 82:6` → `Ps 82:6-7`. The note's "who will die like mortals" is v. 7.
- **ezion-geber**: `2Kgs 16:6` note "Elath lost to Edom (MT reads Aram; Qere 'Edomites')" → "Elath lost to Edom (Kethiv 'Arameans', Qere 'Edomites'; 'Aram' in v. 6a often emended to 'Edom')". The old note set "MT" against "Qere", but both are MT. The disagreement is Kethiv ʾrmym vs. Qere ʾdwmym.
- **fall-of-babylon-539**: `Isa 21:1-10` now has `disputed: true`. Note → "'Fallen, fallen is Babylon'; Elam and Media attack; 539 or an earlier Assyrian-era fall debated". Some scholars date the oracle to 539; others connect it to the Assyrian-era sieges or sacks of Babylon (710–689).
- **forgeries-in-biblical-archaeology**: `Exod 28:33-34` note "…the ivory pomegranate's inscription is forged" → "…is widely judged forged". The IAA committee's verdict of forgery is still contested by some epigraphers, so the flat claim overstated it.
- **four-room-house**: removed `2Sam 11:2 | realia | David walks on the palace roof`. It is a palace roof, not a domestic pillared house, so the connection was trivially weak. The flat-roof point is already covered by Deut 22:8, Josh 2:6, 1 Sam 9:25-26 and 2 Kgs 4:10.
- **el**: `Deut 32:8-9` note "Elyon apportions the nations among the sons of God" → "… (4QDeutj; MT 'sons of Israel')". The note relied on the Qumran/LXX reading without saying so, and the English text shows "children of Israel".
- **esarhaddon-treaty-with-baal-of-tyre**: `Ezek 27:25-36` note cut from 23 words to 18 words, which is within the 20-word limit: "Tyre as a laden ship wrecked by the east wind; cf. the treaty's storm-god curse on Tyre's ships". The content is unchanged.

## Flag for expert review

- **dream-incubation** `1Kgs 3:4-15` (`practice`, not marked disputed): Gibeon is the usual biblical candidate for incubation, but whether it was a sought dream rather than a royal dream report is debated (Husser, Gnuse). Consider `disputed: true`.
- **first-jewish-revolt** `Dan 9:26-27`: Josephus, *Ant.* 10.276, says Daniel wrote of the Roman destruction, but he doesn't name Dan 9. Linking that remark specifically to 9:26-27 is an interpretation.
- **elephantine-passover-letter**: the tags assume the festival is Passover/Unleavened Bread. The word "Passover" is restored in TAD A4.1, but the 14th–21st Nisan dates make the identification secure.
- **ebla** `Num 21:29`: Kamiš (Chemosh) at Ebla is widely cited from personal names and a month name. Check against current Eblaite onomastic studies.
- **egyptian-loanwords** `Exod 2:3`: listing gōmeʾ ("papyrus") alongside the secure loans tēbâ, sûp and yĕʾōr may overstate how certain it is.
- **extispicy** `Ps 27:4`: Mowinckel's reading of lĕbaqqēr as cultic inspection (cf. bārû) is a minority view. It is already marked `language?`.
- **egyptian-hittite-peace-treaty** `1Kgs 3:1`: diplomatic marriage is relevant to the entry only through the later Hittite marriage of Ramesses II, which the note doesn't mention. Kept, but the link is loose.
