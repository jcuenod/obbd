# Passage-tag verification: batch 5

Checked 83 entries (judaea-capta-coins … milkom) against the WEB text with `./obbd check-passages --per 2`.
Changed 14 entries: 14 tags edited, 1 removed, 0 added (one edit extends a ref with a second verse).

## Changes

- **justice-and-righteousness**: `2Sam 8:15` note "administered justice and equity" → "executed justice and righteousness (mišpāṭ ûṣĕdāqâ)". The verse has the *mišpāṭ/ṣĕdāqâ* pair, not *mêšārîm*, and "equity" muddied the entry's own Ps 99:4 *mêšārîm* tag.
- **kirta-epic**: `Deut 20:7` → `Deut 20:7; 24:5`, note "Newlywed exempt from war" → "Betrothed and newlywed men exempt from war…". Deut 20:7 concerns a betrothed man; the newlywed exemption is Deut 24:5, which is the closer contrast to Kirta's levy of the bridegroom. `Deut 23:21-23`: added "(MT 23:22-24)".
- **kuntillet-ajrud**: `Deut 33:2` rel `language` → `concept`. Teman does not occur in the verse, so the link is to YHWH's southern origin, not to a word (this matches the sister entry kuntillet-ajrud-inscriptions).
- **kuntillet-ajrud-inscriptions**: `2Sam 15:7` added `disputed: true`. Reading "YHWH-in-Hebron" as a local manifestation (McCarter) depends on contested syntax.
- **laodicea**: `Rev 3:15-16` added `disputed: true`. The hot springs / cold water / tepid aqueduct reading is widely repeated but is contested (e.g. Koester, Porter).
- **lion-hunt-of-ashurbanipal**: `Dan 6:16-24` note "by a Mesopotamian king" → "by the king in Babylon". In the story the king is Darius the Mede, not a Mesopotamian.
- **marduka-tablet**: `Esth 2:5` note "Mordecai (= Babylonian Marduka)…" → "Mordecai, a Judean at the Susa court; possibly the official Marduka of the tablet". The old wording treated a contested identification as fact.
- **melqart**: `Matt 17:24-27` note "Temple tax paid in Tyrian shekels…" → "Temple tax, normally paid in Tyrian shekels…". The passage names only didrachma and stater; the Tyrian coin is inferred from later sources.
- **memphis**: `Jer 2:16` note "have shaved Judah's head" → "have broken (or shaved) the crown of Judah's head". MT *yirʿûk qodqōd* means "graze/break the crown"; "shave" rests on an emendation.
- **menahem**: `2Kgs 15:14-16` rel `event` → `attests`. No extrabiblical source narrates the coup or Tiphsah; the Assyrian sources only name Menahem. `1Chr 5:26` note "Pul identified with Tiglath-pileser" → "Pul and Tilgath-pilneser named together with a singular verb; both are Tiglath-pileser III". The verse names both kings and does not itself equate them.
- **menorah**: `1Macc 1:21; 4:49-50` rel `event` → `realia`. The entry is about the object, not a source for the event.
- **merneptah-stele**: `Gen 32:28` note: added "(MT 32:29)".
- **mesha-stele**: removed `Judg 3:12-30 | setting | Earlier Moabite domination under Eglon…`. The connection is trivially weak, and the note was inaccurate: Eglon's occupation is of the "city of palms" (Jericho region), not the Transjordanian plateau the stele concerns.
- **marriage-and-bride-price**: `Tob 7:13` → `Tob 7:13-14`, note → "Raguel writes a marriage contract (NRSV 7:13; 7:14 in GI-based versions)". In WEB (GI) 7:13 is only the handing over of Sarah, and the written contract is 7:14. NRSV (GII) has it at 7:13.

## Flag for expert review

- **kadesh-battle-reliefs**: `Num 2:1-34` and `Exod 26:15-33` (both `?`) rest largely on Hoffmeier's camp/tent comparison. Kept, but confirm whether they deserve a place.
- **judean-pillar-figurines**: `Gen 31:19, 34-35` (teraphim) is a loose link to the figurines. Kept as `practice?`.
- **low-chronology-debate**: the David/Solomon narrative tags use `attests?` and `event?` for passages whose historicity is the very subject of the debate. Consider whether `setting` is the better relation throughout.
- **mesha-stele**: `2Sam 8:2` is used to anchor the line 31 *bt[d]wd* reading. The reading relates to "House of David" generally rather than to this verse; a reviewer may prefer to drop it.
- **mari / mari-letters**: Benjamin ~ Binu-Yamina (`Gen 35:18`) is tagged `language` in mari but `language?` in mari-letters. The onomastic parallel is secure but a historical link is not, so the two should be harmonised.
- **king-as-son-of-god**: `Mark 1:11` is tagged `imagery` while `Heb 1:5` (same Ps 2:7 reception) is `concept`. Arguably both should be `concept`.
- **library-of-ashurbanipal**: `Ezra 4:10` Osnappar = Ashurbanipal is the standard view but is not universal.
- **laodicea**: `Rev 3:17-18` (banking, black wool, Phrygian eye-salve) follows Ramsay and Hemer. It is kept undisputed, but the eye-salve link in particular has been questioned.
