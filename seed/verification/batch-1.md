# Verification batch 1

Checked 83 entries (4qdeut-j … beni-hasan-asiatics) against the WEB text with `./obbd check-passages --per 2`. All MT versification notes in the batch were checked and are correct (e.g. Ps 44:23-24 = MT 44:24-25; Deut 23:3-6 = MT 23:4-7; Hos 2:8-9 = MT 2:10-11; Ezek 21:22 = MT 21:27; Dan 5:30-31 = MT 5:30-6:1; Mal 4:5-6 = MT 3:23-24). I found no off-by-one or wrong-chapter references. 11 entries changed.

## Changes

- **akitu-festival**: `Zech 14:16-17` concept → concept + `disputed: true`. Note "Nations go up yearly to worship the King at Booths" → "…; autumn enthronement-festival hypothesis". The link to an akītu-type festival is the same Mowinckel hypothesis already marked disputed on the enthronement psalms in this entry.
- **akhenaten**: removed `Josh 10:1` (setting, "Jerusalem's ruler writes to Akhenaten (Abdi-Heba, EA 285-290)"). The verse concerns Adoni-zedek, not Akhenaten. The connection runs only through the Amarna letters, where it is already tagged (amarna-letters, Josh 10:1-5).
- **ane-wisdom-literature**: `Prov 30:1` rel attests → concept, and the note "Words of Agur son of Jakeh: a non-Israelite sage" → "…, often taken as a non-Israelite (Massaite) sage". The entry does not attest Agur, and his non-Israelite origin rests on reading *hammaśśāʾ* as a gentilic. `Prov 31:1` rel attests → concept, for the same reason.
- **apkallu-reliefs**: `Gen 5:21-24` note "Enoch, seventh antediluvian, paired with the sage tradition of Enmeduranki" → "Enoch, seventh antediluvian, compared with seventh king Enmeduranki and seventh sage Utuabzu, who ascended". Enmeduranki is an antediluvian king, not an apkallu. The sage parallel is Utuabzu, who ascended to heaven (Bīt mēseri).
- **arad-ostraca**: `Gen 10:4` note "Kittim, here apparently Aegean or Cypriot mercenaries in Judahite service" → "Kittim, who receive rations in the ostraca; probably Greek or Cypriot mercenaries in Judahite service". "Here" wrongly suggested that Gen 10:4 itself refers to mercenaries.
- **ashurbanipal**: `2Kgs 21:1` rel setting → attests. Note "Manasseh among his western vassals" → "Manasseh of Judah, listed among Ashurbanipal's western vassals (Prism C)". Ashurbanipal's annals name Manasseh (Minsê), which is attestation, not setting.
- **assyrian-sacred-tree**: `Ezek 31:2-9` note "Assyria as a great cedar in the garden of God (MT 31:3 'Assyria')" → "Great cedar in the garden of God; 31:3 reads 'Assyria' (often emended 'cypress')". In OBBD style, "MT x:y" signals a versification difference, and there is none here. The point is the MT reading ʾaššûr, which is often emended to *teʾaššûr*.
- **assyrian-eponym-list**: removed `Amos 1:1` (setting, "Prophet dated by kings and an earthquake, a different dating system"). It is trivially weak, because the verse has no connection to the limmu lists beyond being a dating formula.
- **athtar**: `Isa 14:12-15` note shortened from 21 to 20 words: "…and is cast down; Athtar's failed enthronement compared" → "…and falls; cf. Athtar's failed enthronement".
- **babylon**: `Gen 11:1-9` note "The tower of Babel; wordplay on the name (Akk. bāb-ilim 'gate of god')" → "The tower of Babel; name explained by bālal 'confuse', not Akk. bāb-ilim 'gate of god'". The old note implied that the biblical wordplay was on *bāb-ilim*, but it is on *bālal*.
- **battle-of-qarqar**: `1Kgs 20:31-34` setting → setting + `disputed: true`. Note "Treaty between Ahab and Ben-hadad" → "Treaty between Ahab and Ben-hadad, often linked to the Qarqar alliance; chapter's setting debated". This makes it consistent with the ahab entry, which marks 1 Kgs 20 as possibly concerning later kings. `1Kgs 16:29` note "Ahab, king of Israel, dated by the battle" → "Ahab, named in the Kurkh Monolith's Qarqar account, which places his reign's end near 853".

## Flag for expert review

- **adapa** `Gen 5:21-24` (parallel?): Adapa's ascent to Anu compared with Enoch. The Enoch comparison is more usually with Enmeduranki or Utuabzu. I kept it as disputed.
- **akkadian-prophecies** `Ezek 43:1-5`, `Ezek 10:18-19; 11:22-23` (parallel, not disputed): the divine-abandonment and return motif from the Marduk Prophecy (Block and others). Consider whether these should be marked disputed.
- **battle-of-qarqar** `1Kgs 22:1` and `1Kgs 22:39`, and **ahab** `1Kgs 22:1`: the "three years without war" and the "silence of Kings" tags are inferential. I kept them because they reflect standard discussion, but they are thin.
- **aqhat-epic** `Isa 1:17` (language): Danel's judging of widow and orphan. It might be better as `practice` (royal justice) than `language`.
- **amel-marduk** and **babylonian-chronicles** `Dan 5:30-31` (event): the event is the same (the fall of Babylon), but Daniel's account (Belshazzar killed, "Darius the Mede") conflicts with the Nabonidus Chronicle. The note does not overclaim, but a specialist may want this marked disputed.
- **adoption** `Gal 4:5`: "Roman legal idiom". A Greco-Roman versus Jewish (covenantal) background for *huiothesia* is debated (e.g. J. M. Scott). The note may be slightly too definite.
- **amarna-letters** `Josh 11:10`: the Hazor correspondence (EA 227-228, 148). The text is fine, but the claim that the ruler "corresponds with Egypt" rests on these letter numbers, which should be verified.
- **arad** `Ezra 2:44` (attests?): the "Kerosite" identification in the Arad ostraca (Arad 18) should be checked against the edition.
